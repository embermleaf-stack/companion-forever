## Findings common to all 15 files

- Every file is **1024x1024, 24-bit RGB, no alpha channel**. Every brief with a non-square target (480x360, 800x600, 2400x900, 1280x640, 600x120) therefore needs a crop or a regenerate at the right aspect ratio. Nothing is transparent; "transparent background" came out as opaque white (or full-bleed paper).
- **Stray text:** the pasted job titles did not render as text. One case of legible lettering: ART-005 has "Error" written on the ink-pot label. ART-007 has illegible pseudo-handwriting in the ledger. No watermarks, logos, signatures or game marks were seen.
- Style: pastoral watercolor/gouache is matched on most. ART-013 and ART-014 read more like digital cinematic or anime matte paintings than flat gouache; ART-012 is loose ink wash.
- No recognizable game or film characters, creatures or emblems seen. Checking is by eye only; no reverse-image search was possible.
- Palette is broadly Emberleaf (ember, bronze, parchment, leaf). ART-006, ART-011, ART-012 and the sky of ART-013/014 are blue-teal dominant; acceptable as accents, not as a brand color.
