# UI kit brief: AI-generated interface art (optional)

Status: proposed, 2026-11-07. Docs only. No art has been generated for this brief. Not a legal clearance: the output terms and ownership of any generator are UNVERIFIED (see "Tool terms" below).

Always supply [STYLE-GUIDE.md](STYLE-GUIDE.md) (palette, pastoral hand-painted direction, "do not" list) with each prompt below. Job ids are UI-001 to UI-006 (one per item below). Queue entries live in [art-queue.md](art-queue.md) and copy-paste prompts in [prompts-copy-paste.md](prompts-copy-paste.md).

## Why this exists, and the fallback

The app already ships hand-authored interface art, so nothing below is required:

- Button sprites: `icons-and-imagery/final/btn-{primary,secondary,danger,disabled}*.svg` (48 px, 9-slice source, generated by `icons-and-imagery/final/build-button-sprites.mjs`), rasterized to `apps/mobile/assets/art/ui/btn-*.png` (1x/2x/3x).
- UI glyph sprite: `icons-and-imagery/final/ui-sprite.svg`.
- Art registry: `apps/mobile/src/art.ts` and `apps/web/src/art.ts`. Each entry has a job id, asset, alt text, `provisional` flag and `enabled` flag.

AI-generated versions are optional and swappable. To replace a hand-authored asset, add the new file, point its registry entry at it, and keep alt text. If the AI result fails the checklist, set `enabled: false` or keep the existing file. The UI must work with no AI art at all.

## Shared rules

- **Shared negative prompt** (paste into the separate negative field, or append as `Avoid: ...` if the tool has none):

  `text, letters, logo, watermark, signature, figures, people, faces, photorealistic, 3d render, glossy highlights, lens flare, glowing drop shadow, flaming border, dragon-scale border, brand name, named artist, film still, recognizable character, existing game character, game or studio trade dress`

  "Low contrast" is deliberately left out of this list. The background tile (UI-006) needs it, and the button states must still read.
- **Style:** pastoral hand-painted anime-fantasy look, watercolor and gouache, clean expressive ink linework, soft colour fills, warm lantern light from the upper left. No gloss, no glow halos, no 3D.
- **Palette (Emberleaf):** ink `#14171F`, slate `#1F2430`, vellum `#E8DCC0`, ember `#E0702B`, leaf `#5E9E5A`, bronze `#B58A4A`, candle `#F6D98A`.
- **Names:** never name an artist, studio, film or game in a prompt, filename or note. Describe the look only.
- **Original design only.** Reject anything that resembles an existing game's UI, frame, button or ornament. When in doubt, regenerate or drop it.
- **Grids are a request, not a guarantee.** Generators do not honour pixel grids. Generate at any square size, then crop, resize and slice to the exact sizes below.
- **Staging only.** Outputs go to `icons-and-imagery/staging/` with the job id in the name (for example `UI-001_buttons_v1.png`). Nothing moves to `final/` without a provenance entry and owner approval.

## Items

Sizes are exact export sizes. "9-slice" means the image is split into 9 regions, the corners stay fixed and the edges and centre stretch.

### UI-001: Button sprites (primary, secondary, danger; normal, pressed, disabled)

- **Purpose:** GameButton and similar controls. Primary = Ember, secondary = Aged Bronze with Slate, danger = Rust.
- **Output:** one sheet, `ui-btn-sheet.png`, 768 x 384 px, transparent background. Fixed grid of 3 columns (primary, secondary, danger) by 3 rows (normal, pressed, disabled); each cell 256 x 128 px. Each button is a centred 224 x 80 px rounded rectangle inside its cell. Slice each button as 9-slice with left and right caps 24 px, top and bottom caps 20 px; the centre stays flat.
- **Prompt:**

```text
Hand-painted pastoral fantasy UI button set in watercolor and gouache: three rounded-rectangle buttons side by side with soft wash fills and clean expressive ink outlines, thin Aged Bronze double-line edge, soft inner shadow. Left to right: primary button in Ember orange, secondary button in Aged Bronze with slate, danger button in muted Rust. Top row normal, middle row pressed (slightly darker, inset shadow), bottom row disabled (desaturated, flat, pale vellum). Fixed 3 by 3 grid, even spacing, transparent background. Each button is symmetric with a flat empty centre and nothing inside it. Original design, no text, no letters, no figures.
```

### UI-002: Panel and card border with corner ornaments (9-slice)

- **Purpose:** card and panel frame. The centre is transparent so content sits inside.
- **Output:** `ui-panel-9slice.png`, 96 x 96 px, transparent centre. Corner ornaments fill the four 24 x 24 corner regions. Slice with 24 px caps on all sides; the middle of each edge is 48 px and repeats or stretches.
- **Prompt:**

```text
Hand-painted pastoral fantasy panel frame in gouache and watercolor: a rounded card border with clean expressive ink linework and a soft Aged Bronze fill, Old Vellum inner edge, small leaf-and-vine ornaments in the four corners, simple rivets along the straight edges. Flat, straight-on view, symmetric on all four sides, straight edges that repeat cleanly along each side. Transparent empty centre, flat, nothing inside the frame. Palette: Slate Ledger, Aged Bronze, Leaf green accents, Candle highlight only in the corner ornaments. Original design, no text, no letters, no figures.
```

### UI-003: Ornamental divider rule

- **Purpose:** section separator in lists and About.
- **Output:** `ui-divider.png`, 512 x 32 px, transparent background. Horizontal stretch: 64 px caps at each end, the centre is a 1 px column that repeats across the width. The leaf sprig sits at the centre.
- **Prompt:**

```text
Hand-painted pastoral fantasy ornamental divider rule in watercolor and gouache: a thin horizontal line of Aged Bronze with a small leaf sprig at the centre and a tiny Ember dot, tapering to fine ends, clean expressive ink linework, soft wash fill. Straight, horizontal, perfectly symmetric left to right, centred on a transparent background. One row only, nothing else on the canvas. Palette: Aged Bronze, Leaf green, Ember dot. Original design, no text, no letters, no figures.
```

### UI-004: Tab-bar item frames (active, inactive)

- **Purpose:** bottom tab-bar items. Active and inactive differ by shape as well as colour.
- **Output:** `ui-tab-sheet.png`, 512 x 160 px, transparent background. Two cells side by side, each 256 x 160 px. Left cell = active, right cell = inactive. Each tab is a centred 224 x 128 px shape. 9-slice caps: 20 px left and right, 16 px top and bottom.
- **Prompt:**

```text
Hand-painted pastoral fantasy tab-bar item frames in gouache and watercolor: two rounded tab shapes side by side on a fixed grid. Left tab is active: taller, with a rounded top, filled with Old Vellum parchment, a warm Candle gold glow along the top edge and a soft Ember underline. Right tab is inactive: slightly shorter, flat Slate Ledger with a thin Aged Bronze outline and no glow. Clean expressive ink linework, soft wash fills, flat empty centres for icons, symmetric shapes, transparent background. Original design, no text, no letters, no figures.
```

### UI-005: Checkbox and toggle sprites (on and off)

- **Purpose:** checklist checkboxes and settings toggles. Done versus not done must differ by shape (check mark versus empty box; knob side), not only colour.
- **Output:** `ui-control-sheet.png`, 512 x 256 px, transparent background. 2 x 2 grid, each cell 256 x 128 px. Cells: top-left unchecked box, top-right checked box, bottom-left toggle off, bottom-right toggle on. Boxes are 64 x 64 px centred; toggles are 112 x 56 px centred. Toggles are 9-slice with 28 px caps on left and right.
- **Prompt:**

```text
Hand-painted pastoral fantasy checkbox and toggle set in gouache and watercolor on a fixed 2 by 2 grid. Top left: an empty square checkbox with an Aged Bronze outline. Top right: the same box filled Leaf green with a clean ink check mark. Bottom left: a pill toggle in the off state, Slate track, small bronze knob on the left. Bottom right: the on state, Ember track, Candle gold knob on the right. Clean expressive ink linework, soft wash fills, flat, symmetric, transparent background, each item centred in its cell. The check mark and knob position show the state by shape. Original design, no text, no letters, no figures.
```

### UI-006: Seamless dark parchment and slate background tile

- **Purpose:** app background. Very low contrast, so 4.5:1 text contrast is preserved.
- **Output:** `bg-slate-tile.png`, 512 x 512 px, opaque, seamless on all four edges. Dark variant only. Target luminance is close to slate `#1F2430` with a faint vellum tint.
- **Prompt:**

```text
Seamless tile of a dark parchment and slate background in watercolor and gouache: subtle Slate Ledger paper grain with faint wash mottling and barely visible hand-drawn compass lines, very low contrast, deep blue-grey with a faint warm vellum tint. Edges match exactly on all four sides so the tile repeats without seams. Uniform value, no vignette, no focal object, no bright spots, watercolor paper texture only. Original design, no text, no letters, no figures, no logo.
```

## Post-processing (all items)

Firefly connector capabilities are limited: it can crop, resize, remove a background, adjust and expand existing images. It cannot generate images from text. Generation must be done by the owner in an external tool (the Firefly web app, another tool with an API, or a human illustrator). Before any connector call, run `adobe_mandatory_init` as the connector instructions require.

1. Crop to the grid cells or to the single shape, then resize to the exact sizes above (Lanczos or bicubic for scaling down).
2. Remove the background if the tool did not output transparency. Use `image_remove_background` (connector) or a manual mask. Check the edge for halo fringing.
3. Slice where the item is 9-slice or stretch-able. Check that the caps and the stretch regions match the stated insets.
4. Seam test for UI-002 edges and UI-006: offset the image by half its width and height and look for seams. Fix seams by hand; do not accept a tile that shows one.
5. Optional: quantize to 8-bit PNG if the file is over 300 KB (the image-file limit in IMAGERY.md).
6. Export PNG (or WebP) to `icons-and-imagery/staging/` only. Log the tool and job in `credit-ledger.md` and each output in `provenance.md`.

## Acceptance checklist

- [ ] Edges are clean, with no fringe, halo or cut-off shapes
- [ ] Symmetric left to right (and top to bottom for UI-002, UI-006)
- [ ] No baked-in text, letters, logos or watermarks
- [ ] Centre is flat where the item is 9-slice (buttons, panel, tab, toggle)
- [ ] Tiles seamlessly where relevant (UI-002 edges, UI-003 stretch, UI-006 tile)
- [ ] Legible at 48 px: each state reads by shape, not by colour alone
- [ ] Disabled and pressed states are distinguishable from normal
- [ ] Palette matches the Emberleaf values in STYLE-GUIDE.md
- [ ] Nothing from the "do not" list in STYLE-GUIDE.md; no game or studio trade dress
- [ ] Provenance entry filled (PROVENANCE-TEMPLATE.md), alt text written
- [ ] Owner approval recorded before anything reaches `final/` or `apps/*`
